# Scritto Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 2026-10-10 · App: Scritto (`com.internship.scritto`)_

> **Before publishing:** fill in the contact email below and host this text at a public URL
> (for example the GitHub Pages or raw page of this repo). Google Play requires a working URL,
> and it must describe the usage statistics below, because the app collects them automatically.

## The short version

- Your notes, tasks, schedule and imported files stay on your phone. Scritto has no account and no sign-in.
- If you use **Scritto AI**, what you ask it (and any file you attach) is sent to Google's Gemini service to get a reply.
- Scritto sends **anonymous usage statistics**: a random ID and the names of features used. Never the content of your
  notes, tasks, files or chats.

## Data that stays on your device

Notes, tasks, events and classes, imported files, edited copies of files, and your settings are stored
locally. We cannot see them and they are not uploaded by Scritto.

## Scritto AI (Google Gemini)

When you send a message to Scritto AI or use the voice assistant, the app sends to Google's Gemini API:
your message, the parts of your workspace the assistant needs to answer (for example matching note titles,
task and event details), and the text or contents of any file you attach or ask it to read.
Google processes this under its own terms and privacy policy. Please don't attach anything you are not
comfortable sending. Voice input uses your device's speech recognition service.

## Anonymous usage statistics

Scritto automatically collects:

| Data | Example | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Random installation ID | `3f2b…` (a random UUID made on first launch) | Count unique installs without knowing who you are |
| App version and Android version | `1.5`, `36` | Know which versions are in use |
| Feature events with a timestamp | `note_created`, `task_completed`, `voice_started`, `document_opened` | See which features are used |

Scritto **never** collects: the text of notes, tasks, events, files or AI chats; file names; contacts;
location; advertising ID; or anything that identifies you.

Events are queued on your phone and uploaded over HTTPS in the background. We do not sell this data or
share it for advertising; it is only used to improve the app.

**Stopping it:** uninstalling Scritto, or clearing its storage, deletes the installation ID from your phone.
Uploaded data is tied only to that random ID, so it can't be linked back to you after that. To ask us to delete
the data tied to your ID, contact us (see below) with the ID, which we can't look up for you.

## Children

Scritto is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
